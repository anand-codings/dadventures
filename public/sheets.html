Arrangement
Laminating: keep the gap at ¼–½ in so every piece keeps a sealed border. Cutting on a guillotine without laminating: set the gap to 0 so each cut line is shared, and a margin of ⅜ in so the crop marks at the page edges print (printers cannot print the outer ~0.2 in).
Double-sided
Each front page is followed by a mirrored back page, so every back lands exactly behind its front. Give each piece a back by dropping an image on its back box (or on the back page in the preview). Pieces without a back print blank on the reverse. Ask for “double-sided, flip on the long edge”.
